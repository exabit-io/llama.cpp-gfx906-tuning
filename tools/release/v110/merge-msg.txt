274 upstream commits on top of b12500. 19 files had textual conflicts.

The one decision in this merge: upstream replaced the snapshot rollback of
the delta-net models by a replay (#31669: one state plus the inputs of the
last ubatch, the gdn op replays the kept tokens before the new ones) and
removed the snapshot branch. The fork has the snapshot ring for the same
job, and plane-indexed snapshots for the contexts the ring does not cover.
Everything is kept, one rollback per context:
  - a context the ring covers (one sequence, verified backends) keeps the
    ring and its snapshots, unchanged;
  - every other context of these models with n_rs_seq > 0 keeps the
    plane-indexed snapshots by default. The fork's gated delta net kernels
    do not replay, so a replay op runs on the recurrent kernel;
  - LLAMA_RS_REPLAY=1 gives those contexts upstream's replay, which keeps
    a quarter of the recurrent memory.
llama_memory_recurrent::ring_supported() is the ring's condition as one
static function, used by the memory and by llama_context.
