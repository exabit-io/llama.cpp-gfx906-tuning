# Documentation debt after the 2026-09-19..22 campaign

What is still not documented, or is documented wrongly, after ~20 GPU hours. Written down because
the pattern in this campaign was that *undocumented* results and *stale* documents caused more damage
than any measurement error: a wrong-signed coefficient in `optimize.py` steered recommendations away
from a real win for two weeks, and a "do not re-test" line in `CLAUDE.md` closed off a capacity mode
that works.
